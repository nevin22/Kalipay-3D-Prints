# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Gift buyers and social-media visitors, often arriving from Kalipay Prints 3D posts on Facebook and often on a phone. They may not own a 3D printer. They want to personalize something small (a keycap with a name, initials or a favorite word) for themselves or someone else. Printer owners who open the downloaded 3MF in a slicer (Bambu Studio is the owner's) are a secondary audience, not confirmed as equal.

## Product Purpose

Kalipay 3D Prints is a free web app. Today it contains the Keycap Fidget Lab: the user designs a mechanical keyboard keycap with a text legend, sees it live in 3D, and downloads a two-color 3MF for printing. Success is that a first-time visitor gets a usable, printable design with no friction.

## Positioning

Free, with no accounts, payments, memberships or "pro" features in v1. A fidget keycap plus a switch housing (with an optional keychain ring), so the cap works as a desk fidget without a keyboard. Part of a growing, playful range under one brand.

## Operating Context

The output is a .3mf opened in a desktop slicer, so the design happens on the site and the printing happens elsewhere. Download behavior differs on phones and in in-app browsers (Instagram, TikTok may block downloads). The owner works one small step at a time and confirms each step in the browser.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keycaps: up to 6 keys in one row, text legend, size/offset/rotation/raise, cap height, stem tolerance, housing layout and ring, live 3D, two-color 3MF export.
- Keychains and Vases are planned and shown as "Coming soon"; they are not built.
- No SVG upload (the owner does not want it). A built-in icon library, more fonts and print modes are roadmap items.
- Free, one cap at a time, no accounts.
- Open-licensed fonts and icons only (OFL, MIT, Apache), with a credits page.
- Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind 4, three.js; deploy target is Vercel (roadmap, not confirmed done).

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name stays "Kalipay 3D Prints" (Kalipay Prints 3D in social posts; keep the name as the owner writes it on the home title).
- Home title lettering is the Mouldy Cheese font in puffy white letters on orange plates.
- Must work on phones: usable phone layout, touch-sized controls.

## Evidence on Hand

- A Facebook page linked from the home page (https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61581448113902).
- No testimonials, customer counts, gallery or analytics numbers are on hand; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Free and frictionless: no sign-up or paywall in the path to a download.
2. Show the real thing: the preview is the actual geometry that gets exported.
3. Playful and welcoming to people who have never used a slicer.
4. One small, finished feature at a time; unbuilt features say so honestly.
5. Phones are first-class visitors, even though printing happens on desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal standard was confirmed. Touch targets are sized for thumbs on touch screens. Keep text readable and keyboard focus visible.
